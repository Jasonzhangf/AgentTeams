`ui/teams-console/src/mount.ts:57-67` installs a document keydown handler. Measured with a capture listener and drawer counting:

```
[58222] initial:            drawer=0 overlay=1 active=body
[58222] drawer open:        drawer=1 overlay=1 active=header.teams-drawer-header
[58222] after Escape:       drawer=0 overlay=1 active=button…secondary focusKey=agent:provider:details
[58222] after 2nd Escape:   drawer=0 overlay=0 active=body
[7392]  initial:            drawer=0 overlay=1 active=body
[7392]  drawer open:        drawer=1 overlay=1 active=body
[7392]  after Escape:       drawer=0 overlay=1 active=body
[7392]  after 2nd Escape:   drawer=0 overlay=0 active=body
```

Keyboard Escape closes the drawer and a second Escape closes the console. The earlier claim that Escape did nothing was a measurement error: the probe counted `.teams-overlay`, which remains after only the drawer closes. No Escape defect.

## Projection update cadence and DOM rebuild

Authenticated SSE capture (`sse-frames-control-7392.txt`) produced 6 frames in about 6 seconds while the observed state did not change. Field-by-field diff of the first two frames:

```
CHANGED /projection/connections[0]/status/observedAt | '2026-10-25T00:28:07.815Z' -> '2026-10-25T00:28:08.816Z'
CHANGED /seq | 1 -> 2
```

No other projection field differs. Source: `runtime/control-process.ts:963-968` dedupes by `JSON.stringify(projection)`, and `runtime/control-process.ts:907` builds the membership view with a fresh `new Date().toISOString()` on each observation. The dedupe therefore sees a different string every poll.

During the same update pattern, Playwright locators repeatedly reported "element was detached from the DOM, retrying" for the connect button. UI rebuild and click detachment are separate impact evidence and are not derived from the frame log. No battery or energy claim is made.
